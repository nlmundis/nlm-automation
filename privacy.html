Privacy Policy

NLM Obsidian Automation. Effective 30 October 2026.

Who uses it

This is a personal tool with one user, its owner. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent, it requests read-only access to his own Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Drive (gmail.readonly, calendar.readonly, drive.readonly). It cannot send, change, or delete anything in those accounts.

What it does with the data

It reads messages, events, and files to build private notes on the owner's own computer. The data and the access token stay on that computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
